Mission & Vision

Small beginnings, strong futures.

VISION STATEMENT

Our Vision

To become Nigeria’s leading support network for premature infants, transforming healthcare accessibility so that financial hardship never stands in the way of a child's survival.

MISSION STATEMENT

Our Mission

To protect and preserve the lives of premature infants across Nigeria by combining professional advocacy with rapid, compassionate emergency intervention. We bridge the gap to critical neonatal care for indigent mothers, ensuring that every fragile life receives the resources, dignity, and medical support needed to thrive.

MISSION

Our Focus

To monitor and reduce the rates of infant disability and death resulting from prematurity in Nigeria.

SUPPORT SERVICES

Support Services

Offers assistance and guidance for indigent and otherwise needy mothers facing emergency situations with premature infants.
