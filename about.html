WHO WE ARE

A foundation built around life.

Based in Ibadan, Nigeria.

The Damaris Foundation for Premature Infants is a dedicated non-governmental organization based in Ibadan, Nigeria, working tirelessly to reduce infant mortality and long-term disability caused by premature birth.

Our mission is deeply inspired by the truth of Job 33:4: “The Spirit of God has made me; the breath of the Almighty gives me life.” We believe that every premature baby fighting for their breath in a neonatal unit is a precious child with a divine purpose.

A premature arrival should never mean an early departure, and financial hardship should never stand in the way of a child’s right to breathe and survive.

THE DAMARIS BELIEF

Through rapid emergency intervention, critical resource coordination, and professional advocacy, the Damaris Foundation serves as a vital lifeline—ensuring that the most fragile infants receive the support, dignity, and specialized care they need to grow strong and thrive.
