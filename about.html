WHO WE ARE

About Us

The Damaris Foundation for Premature Infants is a dedicated non-governmental organization based in Ibadan, Nigeria, working tirelessly to reduce infant mortality and long-term disability caused by premature birth.

Our mission is deeply inspired by the truth of Job 33:4: “The Spirit of God has made me; the breath of the Almighty gives me life.” We believe that every premature baby fighting for their breath in a neonatal unit is a precious creation with a divine purpose. A premature arrival should never mean an early departure, and financial hardship should never stand in the way of a child's right to breathe and survive.

Through rapid emergency intervention, critical resource coordination, and professional advocacy, we bridge the gap to high-quality neonatal care for indigent mothers. By matching medical urgency with profound compassion, the Damaris Foundation serves as a vital lifeline—ensuring that the most fragile infants receive the support, dignity, and specialized care they need to grow strong and thrive.
