WHO WE ARE

Protecting fragile lives with compassion.

A dedicated non-governmental organization based in Ibadan, Nigeria.

The Damaris Foundation for Premature Infants is a dedicated non-governmental organization based in Ibadan, Nigeria, working tirelessly to reduce infant mortality and long-term disability caused by premature birth.

Our mission is deeply inspired by the truth of Job 33:4: “The Spirit of God has made me; the breath of the Almighty gives me life.” We believe that every premature baby fighting for their breath in a neonatal unit is a precious child with a divine purpose.

A premature arrival should never mean an early departure, and financial hardship should never stand in the way of a child’s right to breathe and survive.

THE DAMARIS BELIEF

Through rapid emergency intervention, critical resource coordination, and professional advocacy, the Damaris Foundation serves as a vital lifeline—ensuring that the most fragile infants receive the support, dignity, and specialized care they need to grow strong and thrive.

PURPOSE

Built around a clear
purpose.

Our mission and vision guide the Foundation’s work for premature infants and their families.

01
MISSION

Protect and preserve lives.

To monitor and reduce the rates of infant disability and death resulting from prematurity in Nigeria.

To protect and preserve the lives of premature infants across Nigeria by combining professional advocacy with rapid, compassionate emergency intervention. We bridge the gap to critical neonatal care for indigent mothers, ensuring that every fragile life receives the resources, dignity, and medical support needed to thrive.
02
VISION

A Nigeria where every fragile life has a chance.

To become Nigeria’s leading support network for premature infants, transforming healthcare accessibility so that financial hardship never stands in the way of a child's survival.

To build a Nigeria where no premature infant is lost or limited by a lack of resources, and where every mother has immediate access to life-saving neonatal care.
